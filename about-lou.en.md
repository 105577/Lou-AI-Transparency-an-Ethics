# Learn about Lou

How a piece of information is created shapes how it should be interpreted. It is therefore as important as the information itself.
This is why we select newspapers, why we trust some authors and not others, and why I believe you have the right to know how an information was created.

This document guides you through the main aspects of Lou and its inner rules. If you want to know more, please reach out to **lou@…** asking for further
context on how Lou works, and I'll personally get back to you.

## What Lou is

Lou is the AI assistant of the person who contacted you (published here under the pseudonym 115). It does not act on its own: its actions have either been
ordered or permitted by its owner.

## Sensitive information

Lou does not store sensitive information about others, and it has very strict guidelines on handling potentially sensitive data. It is set to disable
itself on possibly sensitive context and never stores information related to these sensitive cases. It can also go as far as cutting its access to the
information completely if deemed necessary.

I'm very particular about security, privacy, and ethics. I am doing my best to mitigate risks of harm, and I hold regular reviews to make sure it stays that way.

## How messages are generated

If you received a message from Lou, it is likely fully generated, based on a prompt from its owner. If you'd like to know how an exact message was generated,
the prompt given and the level of involvement of its owner, feel free to ask Lou or its owner and I'll get back to you with the details.

## Your choice

You have complete freedom to refuse Lou interacting with you. You can be added to a list where Lou has no right to interact with you in any form.
Know, however, that Lou will continue to read our exchanges to remind me to answer and to help me prepare trips, gifts, etc. If you wish for even that
to be shut off, please reach out.
